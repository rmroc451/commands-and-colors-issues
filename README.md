# Commands & Colors: Tricorne

This public repository hosts player documentation and the issue tracker for the Commands & Colors: Tricorne browser portal. The guides describe the current portal interface and its available play modes; they are not a replacement for the rules in the published board game.

## What you can do

- Browse scenarios and commission a battle in the War Room.
- Play against another commander, play against the Tricorne computer opponent, or spectate.
- Use the battlefield map, command cards, movement and attack previews, combat flow, and command history.
- Follow commander records and community features, including friends, regiments, and tournament tools that are still evolving.

## Report a problem

Use **Report a Bug** in the portal or [open an issue](https://github.com/rmroc451/commands-and-colors-issues/issues/new/choose). Include steps to reproduce the problem, the scenario and game phase, what you expected, and what happened. Issues are public; remove passwords, private messages, and personal information from reports and screenshots.

This is a fan-made digital project. Commands & Colors and the underlying game designs belong to their respective designers and publishers; this portal is not an official product or affiliated service.
